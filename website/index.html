🚀 We're hiring

Help us make Malaysia's food app blow up.

We're building Rasa in public — and we want food-obsessed students and creators to grow it with us. Two paid roles, based around the Klang Valley (remote-friendly). No corporate experience needed, just taste and hustle.

Content Creator Intern

Part-time · Paid · KL / Remote

You live on TikTok and Instagram and can turn a plate of char kway teow into a scroll-stopping video. As our Content Creator Intern you'll shoot and edit the organic content that introduces Rasa to Malaysia — food carousels, short-form videos and "best spots" series that people save and share.

What you'll do

  • Produce 3–5 short-form videos & carousels a week (film, edit, caption)
  • Cover local food spots — hawker stalls, cafés, mamak, hidden gems
  • Jump on trending audio and formats fast
  • Work with the founders on content themes and the posting calendar

What we're looking for

  • A portfolio or personal account showing you can make content people watch
  • Comfortable on camera or behind it (faceless content is welcome)
  • Genuinely into Malaysian food and social trends
  • Reliable, self-starting, and quick to ship

What you get

  • Paid once your content hits impression milestones — starts as an unpaid, portfolio-building role and converts to paid as your posts perform
  • Feature credit + real portfolio material you can show off
  • Full creative ownership of Rasa's social presence
  • A front-row seat building a startup from zero

Campus Ambassador

Flexible · Paid · Your campus

You're the person whose food recs everyone already trusts. As a Campus Ambassador you'll bring Rasa to your university — spreading the word, sharing your referral code, and getting your friends ranking their favourite spots. Paid on results, with room to grow as we scale campus by campus.

What you'll do

  • Represent Rasa on your campus and in your circles
  • Share your unique referral code and invite friends to the app
  • Help early users get started and rank their first few places
  • Feed real student feedback back to the team

What we're looking for

  • Currently at a Klang Valley university (or nearby)
  • Social, well-connected, and trusted for your food takes
  • Active on WhatsApp / Instagram / Telegram campus groups
  • Motivated by hitting goals — this role rewards activation

What you get

  • Paid per activation — the more friends you get eating on Rasa, the more you earn
  • First access to new features and drops
  • Ambassador-only perks
  • A résumé-worthy growth-marketing role at an early-stage startup
