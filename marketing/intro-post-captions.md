## LinkedIn (founder / build post)

Everyone has that one friend whose restaurant recommendations never miss. We're building the app that scales that friend.

Meet **Rasa** — a social food-discovery app for Malaysia.

The problem: aggregate star ratings are noise. A 4.2 from 3,000 strangers tells you nothing about whether *you'll* love a place. Meanwhile the best recs in Malaysia still live in WhatsApp groups and word of mouth.

## Slide 1 — hook bank (A/B test these)
The cover slide carries ~80% of a carousel's performance and you get ~0.25s to stop the
scroll. Rules used: 5–8 word headline, a curiosity gap, a visual pattern-interrupt (the
floating score chips), and it speaks straight to Malaysian foodies. Built default:

> **"Rank every place you've ever eaten."**

Swap-in alternates (all lean into Rasa's core = *ranked, not rated*, à la Beli):
- **"Name your top 5 restaurants. Right now."** — challenge / can't-do-it curiosity gap
- **"You've been to 100+ spots. Where's your list?"** — collector / FOMO identity
- **"Does it even count if it's not on Rasa?"** — Beli's viral status hook, localized
- **"Google reviews don't know your taste."** — contrarian / anti-Yelp
- **"The end of 'up to you lah.'"** — pure Malaysian relatability
